Material 3 references and applied decisions

Cards

Use cards for related content, not every field. Keep surfaces low elevation and let one dominant work area lead.

Menus

Menus hold temporary choices; always-visible actions stay in the toolbar. Existing app menu and Select primitives remain the standard.

Date pickers

Current and selected dates must be clear, targets must remain large enough, and the picker variant should match the task instead of exposing a browser-default control arbitrarily.

Progress indicators

Use one consistent linear or circular status indicator for actual processing. Keep the status near the work it describes and do not leave a page indefinitely ambiguous.

Text fields

Labels stay brief, visible, and actionable; errors explain the correction. Technical tokens and hashes are not ordinary form fields.

Lists

Order information logically, keep rows scannable, and align repeated content and actions consistently. This was also the nearest active M3 guidance for the app’s tabular list surfaces; no active M3 data-table page was available.

Canonical layouts

Use adaptive scaffold, list-detail, and supporting-pane patterns. Focused tasks can layer over the main context; secondary explanations become disclosures.
