# How to Drive Rental Command — A Walkthrough & Cheat-Sheet

*A friendly, plain-language guide for a landlord who knows rentals cold but isn't a "computer
person." Written so you can use the app confidently — and demo it to Dad on Father's Day and help
him get his whole portfolio set up.*

**Where it lives:** the website is at **https://rc.coblesolutions.com** (open it in any browser).
There's also an **Android app** that does the same things, made for snapping photos and replying to
tenants on the go. They're the same system — change something in one place and it shows up in the
other.

> **The one big idea:** you don't fill out long forms. You **take a photo of a piece of paper — a
> lease, a receipt, a rent check — and the app reads it and fills in the details for you.** You just
> look it over and tap **Confirm**. Nothing is ever saved behind your back; the app only proposes,
> you approve.

## ⏱️ 5-Minute First Run (do this once)

1. **Sign in.** Go to https://rc.coblesolutions.com, enter your email + password ("Welcome back"
   screen). Forgot it? There's a **"Forgot password?"** link that emails you a reset.
2. **Choose Live or Sandbox.** The very first time, the app asks. **Sandbox** = a safe demo with
   fake example data to poke around in. **Live** = your real properties. *For setting up Dad's real
   rentals, pick **Live**.* (You can show Dad the Sandbox first if you want a no-risk tour.)
3. **Add your first property** — the fastest way is to **scan a lease**:
   - Tap **Scan / Add** (top of the menu, or the camera button in the phone app).
   - Choose **Lease (Agreement)**, take a clear photo (or drop the PDF), wait a few seconds while it
     reads.
   - It pre-fills the **property address, unit, tenant name, rent, deposit, dates, and late fee**.
     Look it over, fix anything, tap **Confirm**. That **one scan creates the property, the unit, the
     tenant, and the lease all at once.**
4. **Repeat for each lease** you have a copy of. In a few minutes Dad's whole portfolio is in.
5. **Open the Dashboard** to see it all come together — occupancy, who owes rent, and your money
   snapshot.

That's the whole magic: *scan → confirm → it's in the books.*

## The Big Picture

Rental Command keeps your **buildings, tenants, rent, bills, and repairs** in one place, so none of
it lives in a shoebox or in your head. There are **two sides**:

- **The landlord side** (you / Dad) — the full app: properties, money, work orders, messages,
  reports.
- **The tenant portal** (what your renters get) — a stripped-down view where a tenant can **pay
  rent, ask for a repair, and message you**. Tenants only ever see their own lease.

**The daily loop** is built right into the menu, in the order you actually work:

> **Dashboard** (what needs attention today) → **Money** (who paid, what you spent) →
> **Work** (repairs and vendors) → **Messages** (talk to tenants).

## First-Time Setup for Dad — Get the Whole Portfolio In Fast

### Option A (the magic): Scan a lease → it builds everything

This is the headline feature and **it works on the live server right now.**

1. **Scan / Add** → **Lease (Agreement)**.
2. Take a **clear photo** of the lease (multiple pages? the phone app stitches them together; or
   upload a PDF). On the website you can drag-and-drop the PDF.
3. Wait a few seconds — *"Reading your document…"* — while it pulls out the details.
4. It shows you what it found, already filled in:
   - **Property** (address) — and if it's a new address, it offers to **create the property**.
   - **Unit** (e.g., "Apt 2B") — creates it if it's new.
   - **Tenant** (name) — creates the tenant, or links to one you already have.
   - **Lease terms** — lease number, start/end dates, **monthly rent, security deposit, late-fee
     amount, and the rent-due day of the month**.
5. Anything the app is **unsure about is flagged** ("please double-check this"). Tap a field and fix
   it.
6. Tap **Confirm.** The property, unit, tenant, and lease are all created and linked. Done.

**Bulk option:** if Dad has a stack of leases, **Scan / Add → batch** lets you upload several at once
and review them one after another.

> **Tip:** the clearer the photo, the better it reads. Good light, flat page, all four corners in
> frame. If a photo is too blurry to read, the app tells you and lets you type it in — you're never
> stuck.

### Option B (the fallback): Add things by hand

Everything you can scan, you can also type:

- **Properties** → **Add property** (name, type, address). Then open the property and **Add unit**
  (unit #, beds, baths, market rent).
- **Tenants** → **Add tenant** (name, email, phone, emergency contact).
- **Leases** → **New lease** (pick property → unit → tenant; set rent, dates, deposit, late fee,
  rent-due day, status).

> **Already collecting rent before the app?** On a lease's page you can set an **Opening Balance** —
> tell it whether the tenant currently owes you or is paid ahead, and as of what date. It shows up in
> the lease's history so the books start from the right spot.

## Feature-by-Feature Reference

Each entry: **what it does · how to get there · tips.**

### Dashboard

- **What it does:** Your morning glance. Up top: portfolio name, **occupancy %**, and **open
  work-order count**. Then a **Today's Briefing** (a plain-English AI summary of what needs you, with
  tap-through links), a **money snapshot** (Collected / Spent / Kept), and **four KPI cards** —
  **Occupancy %**, **Overdue $**, **Net This Month**, **Open Work Orders**. Below that: latest
  messages, latest work orders, leases expiring in 60 days, and recent activity.
- **How to get there:** It's the home screen after you log in (top of the menu).
- **Tips:** If anyone's behind on rent, you'll see "X tenants behind" with a link straight to the
  list. A **Getting-Started checklist** sits here until your setup is done, and points you to the
  next step.

### Properties & Units

- **What it does:** Your list of buildings. Each row shows name, address, type, status, **# of units**
  and **# occupied**. Open one to see its **units**, its **leases**, and a full **change history**.
- **How to get there:** **Rentals → Properties**. Open a property → **Add unit** for each unit.
- **Tips:** Search/filter by name, type, or status. Editing is inline — tap the pencil, change a
  field, save.

### Tenants

- **What it does:** Everyone renting from you — name, email, phone, how many active leases. Open a
  tenant to edit them, see their leases, and start a notice.
- **How to get there:** **Rentals → Tenants** → **Add tenant**.
- **Tips:** Email + phone matter — they're how the tenant gets the portal, payment links, and texts.

### Leases

- **What it does:** The agreement that ties a tenant to a unit. Shows rent, dates, deposit, late fee,
  rent-due day, and status (**Draft → Active → Notice Given → Expired/Terminated**). Each lease has an
  **Account History** ledger in plain English — "Tenant owes $X," "Paid ahead," or "All caught up" —
  listing every charge and payment.
- **How to get there:** **Rentals → Leases** (or open it from a property/tenant).
- **Tips:** Use **Set Active** when a lease starts, and **Give Notice** (with a move-out date) when
  someone's leaving. You can also **Generate a Lease Agreement** PDF from here.

### Record a Payment (including partial payments)

- **What it does:** Logs rent (and deposits, late fees, utilities) against a lease. Real life isn't
  always paid-in-full, so there's a **Partial** status with an **"Amount Paid" field** — record that
  a tenant paid **$500 of $1,500** and the rest stays owed. Other statuses: Scheduled, Paid, Late,
  Waived.
- **How to get there:** A few ways —
  - **Money** ledger → open a payment → **Mark Paid** (set date, method, reference like a check #).
  - **Scan / Add → Payment** to snap a rent **check** and turn it into a payment.
  - **Money → Past-due** ("Who's behind") → **Mark paid** to clear what a tenant owes in one tap.
- **Tips:** Choose **Partial** to expose the "Amount Paid" box. The method you used last is
  remembered to save typing.

### Log an Expense / Scan a Receipt

- **What it does:** Tracks what you spend, **already sorted into tax categories** (Schedule E:
  Repairs, Utilities, Insurance, Management Fees, etc.). Scan a receipt and it pulls out the vendor,
  total, tax, payment method, and even **individual line items**.
- **How to get there:** **Scan / Add → Expense (Receipt/Bill)**, take a photo, review, **Confirm**.
  Or add one by hand from the **Money** ledger.
- **Tips:** Tie an expense to a property (and a vendor) so your per-property reports and taxes come
  out right. There's a **"Billable to owner"** checkbox if you manage for someone else.

### Work Orders (repairs)

- **What it does:** The full repair lifecycle. A request comes in (you create it, or a **tenant**
  submits it from their portal) → you **assign/dispatch a vendor** → track status
  (**New → Scheduled → In Progress → Waiting for Parts → Completed**) → **pay** the vendor (logged as
  an expense with its receipt) → and you can **rate the vendor** afterward. Add **before/after
  photos** and notes along the way; every step shows on a timeline the tenant can also see.
- **How to get there:** **Work → Work Orders** → **New work order** (pick property/unit/tenant,
  title, priority, vendor). Open one to change status, dispatch a vendor, or call them.
- **Tips:** Priority **Emergency** stands out in red and counts on the dashboard. You can navigate to
  the property in Maps or call the vendor right from the work order.

### Vendors

- **What it does:** Your contractor directory — name, trade, contact, star rating + jobs done, and
  tax flags (**1099-eligible, W-9 on file, preferred**).
- **How to get there:** **Work → Vendors** → **Add vendor**.
- **Tips:** Marking who's **1099-eligible** with a **W-9 on file** makes the year-end **Vendor 1099**
  report just work.

### Messages

- **What it does:** A chat thread with each tenant. You pick how each message goes out: **Portal**
  (in-app), **Email**, or **SMS** (text). Conversations show unread badges.
- **How to get there:** **Inbox → Messages**. Start a new thread by picking a tenant, or reply to an
  existing one.
- **Tips:** Portal is always on; Email/SMS default to whatever you set in Settings but you can flip
  them per message. *(SMS texting needs a one-time setup — see the honesty note at the bottom.)*

### Notices (late-fee and others)

- **What it does:** Formal tenant notices: **Late-Rent Notice**, **Lease Renewal Offer**, and
  **Move-Out Reminder** (plus custom). The app can **draft these automatically** — it watches your
  leases and, for example, drafts a renewal offer when a lease is ~60–75 days from ending, or a
  late-rent notice when there's actually an overdue balance. **It only ever creates a draft — nothing
  is sent to a tenant until you approve it.**
- **How to get there:** **Inbox → Tenant Notices**. Hit **Generate drafts** to have it scan and
  propose; or create one from a tenant's page. Edit the wording, pick channels (Portal/Email/SMS),
  and **Send**.
- **Tips:** Each draft tells you *why* it exists ("Lease expires in 60 days"). Late-rent notices only
  appear when money is genuinely owed.

### The Fair-Housing Safety Check (good to know)

- **What it does:** Before a notice (or tenant message) goes out, the app can **scan your wording for
  fair-housing risk** — language that could look like discrimination against a protected class
  ("adults only," "perfect for a single professional," "no kids," etc.). On a notice draft there's a
  **"Check for fair-housing issues"** button; if anything's risky it **flags the exact phrases, says
  why, and suggests a compliant rewrite.** If you try to send risky wording, it pauses and asks you
  to **Edit** or **Send Anyway** — a gentle guardrail, not a hard block.
- **How to get there:** It's built into **Tenant Notices** (and runs behind tenant messages).
- **Tips:** Great safety net, but it's an assistant, not a lawyer. When in doubt, take the suggested
  rewrite.

### Reports (rent roll, taxes, and more)

- **What it does:** Ready-made reports, grouped by topic. The headliners:
  - **Rent Roll** — snapshot of every active lease: property, unit, tenant, rent, deposit, status,
    totals.
  - **Schedule E** — your rental income and expenses broken out **by tax category**, ready for the
    accountant; plus a **Year-End Packet** PDF.
  - Others: Delinquency (aging), Cash Flow, Property P&L, General Ledger, Occupancy, Lease
    Expirations, Security Deposit Register, **Vendor 1099**, Owner Distributions, Work Orders.
- **How to get there:** **Money → Reports**. Schedule E / Year-End live under **Tax**; owner
  statements under **Owners**.
- **Tips:** Scanning receipts into the right category all year is what makes Schedule E painless at
  tax time.

### Settings & Login

- **What it does:** Your account and portfolio preferences. Set the portfolio/company name and
  timezone; choose which reminders go out (rent due, late fee, lease expiry) and on which channels;
  set a daily briefing; manage your **team** and **owners**; change your password (**Settings →
  Security**).
- **How to get there:** **Settings** (bottom of the menu).
- **Tips:** This is also where SMS texting and the daily briefing get turned on/configured.

## What Your Tenants Get (the Tenant Portal)

So you understand what's on the other side. A tenant logs in and sees a **much simpler** app — only
their own lease, never anyone else's. They can:

- **Home / Pay Rent** — see what's due, the due date, and status; tap **Pay now** to pay online by
  card, or **enroll in autopay**. *(Card pay + autopay need the Stripe setup — see the note below.)*
- **View Account History** — the same plain-English ledger of their charges and payments.
- **Maintenance** — **submit a repair request** (title, description, photos) and **watch its
  status** on the same timeline you see. They can't change it — you run the work order.
- **Messages** — chat with you (Portal/Email/SMS).
- **Lease / documents** — view their lease if attached.

What tenants **cannot** do: see other tenants, see your money/expenses/reports, create notices, or
edit a work order. They submit and view; you manage.

> On the website the tenant portal lives at **/portal**; in the phone app a tenant just sees their
> own four tabs (Home, Messages, Maintenance, More).

## 🎁 Demo Script for Father's Day (~5 minutes)

A suggested order to walk Dad through. Have **one of his real leases** (a photo or PDF) handy for the
live scan — that's the wow moment.

1. **Open the Dashboard** *(30 sec).* "This is your morning glance — here's how full you are, who
   owes you, and what needs attention today." Point at occupancy %, the money snapshot, and the
   briefing.
2. **Show his pre-loaded properties** *(45 sec).* Go to **Rentals → Properties**. "These are your
   buildings; open one and you see its units, its leases, and every change ever made." *(If you
   pre-loaded a couple before the visit, this lands.)*
3. **Scan a new lease — live** *(90 sec, the showstopper).* **Scan / Add → Lease**, photograph one of
   his actual leases, and let everyone watch it pull out the address, tenant, rent, deposit, and
   dates. "It just built the property, the unit, the tenant, and the lease from one photo. Look it
   over… **Confirm**." Now it's in his portfolio.
4. **Show a payment** *(45 sec).* Open that lease (or **Money → Past-due**). "When rent comes in, you
   tap **Mark Paid**. And if someone pays part — say $500 of $1,500 — you record a **Partial** and the
   app keeps track of the rest." Point at the **Amount Paid** field.
5. **Show the tenant portal** *(60 sec).* "And here's what *your renters* get — they log in, see only
   their lease, **pay rent**, and **send you a repair request** that turns into a work order on your
   side." Show the Pay-rent screen and the maintenance request.
6. **(Bonus, if time)** Snap a **receipt** to show an expense appear pre-categorized for taxes, or
   open **Reports → Rent Roll** to show the one-click portfolio snapshot.

Keep it to those beats. The scan-a-lease moment is the one that sells it.

## Honesty Note — What's NOT set up yet on the server

Everything above **works on the live server today** — scanning leases/receipts/checks, properties,
units, tenants, leases, payments (including partial), expenses, work orders, vendors, messages,
notices (manual and automatic), the fair-housing check, the dashboard, and all the reports including
Schedule E.

Two things are **built into the app but need one-time extra configuration** before they'll work for
real tenants:

- **Online card rent payments (and tenant autopay) — needs Stripe set up.** The **Pay now** /
  autopay buttons in the tenant portal are wired and ready, but they need a **Stripe account
  connected** (payment keys added to the server) before a tenant can actually be charged. Until then,
  record rent the normal way (Mark Paid / scan the check).
- **SMS text reminders & texting tenants — needs a text provider set up.** The app can send via
  Portal and Email today. **Text messages** need an SMS account (Twilio, SignalWire, Telnyx, or
  Vonage) connected in **Settings** first. Until that's done, the SMS toggle simply won't send;
  Portal + Email still work.

Neither is broken — they just need keys plugged in. Everything else is good to go for the demo and
for getting Dad's portfolio running.
