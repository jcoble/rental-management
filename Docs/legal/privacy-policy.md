# Rental Command — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 18 June 2026**

Rental Command ("Rental Command", "we", "us") is a property-management application
that landlords and property managers use to run their rental business — managing
properties, tenants, leases, rent payments, maintenance, inspections, documents, and
rental applications. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information the app
collects, why we collect it, how it is stored and shared, and the choices you have.

The app is provided by the operator of Rental Command. If you have any questions or
requests about your data, contact us at **jcoble@rentalcommand.net**.

---

## 1. Who this policy covers

Rental Command is a business tool used by a **landlord/property manager** (the
"account holder"). Through the app, the account holder records information about
their **tenants, rental applicants, prospective tenants, property owners, and
vendors**. This policy describes our handling of all of that information.

We do **not** target or knowingly collect data from children. The app is intended
for adults running or participating in a residential-rental relationship.

---

## 2. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information. Not every field applies to every
user — we only collect what is entered into, or generated by, the app.

### a. Account and login information
- Your name and display name, email address, phone number, and a securely hashed
  password used to sign in.
- Account timestamps such as account creation and last sign-in.
- For property owners and management entities: contact details, mailing address,
  and a tax identification number where provided (used for tax statements such as
  1099s).

### b. Tenant, applicant, and prospect information
- Names, email addresses, phone numbers, home/mailing addresses, date of birth, and
  emergency-contact details for tenants and rental applicants.
- Rental-application details: current and prior address, employer, and stated
  monthly income.
- For applicants who use the optional photo-autofill feature, identity and income
  details **extracted from a photo of an ID or pay stub** that the applicant
  uploads.
- Prospective-tenant (showing) names and emails for scheduled appointments.

### c. Financial and payment information
- Rent and charge records, amounts due and paid, payment dates and methods, security
  deposit amounts and deductions, and expense/receipt records.
- For scanned checks: payer name, check number, and bank name.
- **We do not store full payment-card numbers, card security codes (CVV), or full
  bank-account/routing numbers.** Card payments are processed by **Stripe**, and we
  store only Stripe references (such as a customer or payment-method identifier).
  Bank connections are made through a banking-data provider (**Plaid**), and we store
  only **encrypted** access tokens plus a **masked** account number (for example
  "••1234") and the institution/account name.
- At most, the last four digits of a card may be stored when scanned from a receipt.

### d. Background-screening information (with consent)
- When a rental applicant gives **explicit consent**, we may run a background/credit
  screening through a third-party screening provider. We record the consent itself,
  including the date and the IP address it was given from (for compliance with the
  Fair Credit Reporting Act).
- We store the screening outcome in summary form: a coarse credit band (for example
  "Good"/"Fair"), and yes/no flags for criminal- and eviction-record findings, plus
  a recommendation. The full raw report from the provider is retained for
  audit/troubleshooting and is not displayed in full.
- Where required, we generate and store an adverse-action notice.

### e. Documents, scans, photos, and signatures
- Documents and images you upload or scan — for example lease PDFs, receipts, ID/pay-stub
  photos used for applications, and inspection photos.
- Electronic-signature records for leases and other documents, including the signer's
  name and email, the typed and/or **drawn (handwritten) signature image**, consent,
  signing timestamp, and a signing audit trail (IP address and device/browser
  information) as required for legally valid e-signatures.
- These files are stored on **our own application server's file storage**, not in a
  public or third-party cloud storage bucket.

### f. Communications
- The content of messages between landlord and tenant (and legacy portal messages),
  outbound text messages to vendors, and the notifications the app generates.
- Notification delivery preferences. Any messaging-provider credentials are stored
  **encrypted**.

### g. Device and technical information
- A **push-notification token** and your device platform (Android/iOS/web), so we can
  send you notifications.
- Security and audit data tied to authentication, signing, and record changes —
  including IP addresses, device/browser (user-agent) information, sign-in
  timestamps, and an audit log of changes to records (which may include snapshots of
  the personal fields above).

### h. Device permissions (Android)
The Android app may request the following permissions, only for the stated purpose:
- **Camera / Photos** — to take or attach photos of receipts, IDs, inspections, and
  documents.
- **Microphone (record audio)** — to capture voice memos/commands you initiate.
- **Notifications** — to deliver push notifications you have enabled.

You can grant or deny these at any time in your device settings.

## 3. Why we collect it (purpose)

We use the information solely to **operate the Rental Command service for the account
holder**, including to:
- Provide and maintain the app's property-, tenant-, lease-, and payment-management
  features.
- Process rent and deposit payments and connect bank accounts (through our payment
  and banking providers).
- Run rental-application intake and, with consent, applicant screening.
- Generate and store documents, leases, e-signatures, and notices.
- Send notifications and messages you have configured.
- Keep the service secure, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain an audit trail, and
  comply with our legal obligations.

We do **not** use this information for advertising, and the app does **not** contain
ads.

---

## 4. How we share information

We do **not sell** your personal information.

We share information only as needed to operate the service, with:
- **Service providers** acting on our behalf, including:
  - **Stripe** — payment processing.
  - **Plaid** (or a comparable banking-data provider) — bank-account connectivity.
  - **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging / Apple Push Notification service** — push
    notification delivery.
  - **A background-screening provider** — only when an applicant consents to
    screening.
  - An **SMS/messaging provider** — to send text notifications you enable.
- **The account holder** and authorized users of that landlord's account, who can see
  the tenant/applicant/property records they manage.
- **Legal and safety** recipients, where we are required to disclose information by
  law, or to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the service.

If the service is ever transferred as part of a business transaction, information may
be transferred subject to this policy.

---

## 5. How we protect information

- Connections between the app and our servers are **encrypted in transit** (HTTPS/TLS).
- Sensitive secrets — such as bank-provider access tokens and messaging-provider
  credentials — are stored **encrypted at rest**.
- Passwords are stored only as secure cryptographic hashes, never in plain text.
- Access to records is restricted to the account that owns them.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures
to protect your information.

---

## 6. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as the account holder maintains an active
account and the records are needed to provide the service, and as required to meet
legal, tax, accounting, and audit obligations (for example, payment and lease records,
and FCRA screening/consent records). Many records support a soft-delete and are
removed or anonymized when no longer needed. When information is no longer required,
we delete or de-identify it.

---

## 7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or
delete your personal information, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on
consent (such as background screening).

- **Account holders** can edit and delete much of the data directly in the app.
- **Tenants, applicants, and other individuals** whose data a landlord entered can
  contact us, or the landlord who manages their records, to make a request.
- To request access to or **deletion** of your data, or to ask a question about this
  policy, contact us at **jcoble@rentalcommand.net**. We will respond consistent with
  applicable law. Note that we may need to retain certain records to meet legal
  obligations.

---

## 8. Children's privacy

Rental Command is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your
jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you
believe a child's information has been provided to us, contact us so we can remove it.
