# How Rental Command Works

*A plain-English guide to the app that runs your rentals — written for the person using it, not
the person who built it. No computer jargon.*

---

## What it is, in one breath

Rental Command is an app for running your rental properties from your **phone** (and there's a
matching **website** if you'd rather use a computer). It keeps track of your buildings, your
tenants, the rent, the bills, and the repairs — all in one place — so none of it has to live in a
shoebox or in your head.

## The big idea: the computer does the typing for you

The whole point of this app is that **you don't have to fill out long forms**. Instead:

> **Take a picture of a piece of paper, and the app reads it and fills in the details for you.
> You just look it over and tap "Confirm."**

Snap a photo of a receipt — the app figures out the store, the amount, the date, and the category,
and turns it into an **expense** in your books. Snap a picture of a rent check — it turns it into a
**rent payment**. You check that it looks right, tap a button, and it's saved. That's it.

And nothing is ever saved behind your back. **The app only proposes; you're the one who taps
Confirm.** If it got something wrong, you fix it first. Everything can be edited or undone later.

## Where you use it

- **On your phone** (the app) — this is the main way, made for doing things on the go: snapping
  receipts, checking who owes rent, replying to a tenant.
- **On a computer** (the website) — the same information, with more room on the screen for the
  bigger stuff like reports.

They're the same system — change something in one place and it shows up in the other.

## Signing in

You open the app (or the website) and sign in with your email and password. After that it
remembers you. If you ever forget your password, there's a "Forgot password?" link that emails you
a reset.

## Scanning a document (the part that feels like magic)

This is the feature you'll use most. Here's the whole thing, step by step:

1. Tap **Scan**.
2. Tell it what you're holding: **"Receipt / Bill"** or **"Rent Check / Payment."**
3. **Take a photo** (or pick one from your gallery / drop a PDF on the website).
4. For a few seconds you'll see **"Reading your document…"** — the app is pulling out the vendor,
   the amounts, and the dates for you.
5. It shows you what it found, already filled in. Anything it's **unsure about is flagged** with a
   note like *"please double-check this."* Fix anything that's off (just tap the field and type).
6. Tap **Confirm**. The record is created and saved to your books, and the original photo is kept
   attached to it.

If the photo was blurry or it's a kind of document it can't read, it just tells you and lets you
type the details in yourself — you're never stuck.

## Keeping track of your places and people

- **Properties** — your buildings/houses. Each one can have **units** (apartments), with the rent,
  bedrooms, bathrooms, and whether it's occupied or vacant.
- **Tenants** — the people renting from you, with their contact info.
- **Leases** — who's renting which unit, for how long, the monthly rent, the deposit, and when rent
  is due. You can mark a lease active, give notice, etc.

You add these once, and from then on the app uses them everywhere — so when you scan a rent check,
for example, it lets you pick the lease from a friendly list like **"Unit 4B — Jane Smith"** instead
of making you remember a number.

## Money

- **Rent payments** — record what tenants pay; see who's **paid, scheduled, or late**. There's a
  quick **"Mark paid"** button. (Rent checks you scan land here automatically.)
- **Expenses (bills)** — every bill and receipt, sorted into the right tax category. (Scanned
  receipts land here automatically.)
- **The money picture** — at a glance: how much you've **collected**, how much is **outstanding**,
  how much is **overdue**, and your **expenses**. These update on their own as things change.
- **Tax-time report** — a year's income and expenses broken down by category, ready to hand to your
  accountant (you can download it as a spreadsheet). *(It's for reference, not tax advice.)*
- **Owner statements** — if a property has an owner, a yearly statement of income, expenses, your
  management fee, and what's left for the owner — and you can **email it to them** with one tap.
- **Security deposits** — track what you're holding, record deductions, and when you return one the
  app does the math for you (deposit minus deductions = refund).

## Repairs, visits, and to-dos

- **Maintenance** — log repair requests ("work orders"), set how urgent they are, and move them
  along (scheduled → in progress → done). Tenants can also send a request from their side.
- **Appointments** — showings, move-ins, inspections, owner meetings.
- **Inspections** — schedule and record property inspections.

## Messaging your tenants (like texting)

There's a built-in **Messages** area that works like a normal texting app — a list of
conversations, and you tap into one to see the back-and-forth.

- **You** can start a conversation with a tenant about a topic (rent, a repair, etc.), and choose
  how it reaches them: in their **portal**, by **email**, and/or by **text** *(text needs a one-time
  setup before it sends)*.
- **Tenants** can write back from their own login, and their replies show up on your side instantly.
- Your messages and theirs are shown on different sides of the screen, like any messenger.

## Your daily briefing and asking questions

- **Daily briefing** — a short, plain-English summary of what needs your attention today (who's
  late, a lease expiring soon, an urgent repair). Tap an item to jump straight to it.
- **Ask a question** — type a question in plain English like *"Who's late on rent?"* or *"How much
  did I collect this month?"* and it answers from your actual records.

## A few things worth knowing

- **Nothing happens without your OK.** The app suggests; you confirm. Everything is editable and
  reversible.
- **Your stuff is private to you.** You only ever see your own properties, tenants, and money.
- **A couple of things are still being set up:** paying rent by card online (tenants are told
  "coming soon" for now), and **text-message** sending (it needs a one-time phone setup before
  texts actually go out — email already works).

## Quick "how do I…?" cheat sheet

| I want to… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Record a receipt or bill | **Scan → Receipt/Bill →** take a photo → Confirm |
| Record a rent check | **Scan → Rent Check/Payment →** take a photo → pick the lease → Confirm |
| See who owes rent | Open **Payments** (or the **daily briefing**) |
| Mark rent as paid | **Payments →** tap **Mark paid** on that row |
| Add a property or tenant | **Properties** / **Tenants →** the **+** button |
| Log a repair | **Maintenance →** the **+** button |
| Message a tenant | **Messages →** **New** → pick the tenant → write → send |
| See my numbers for taxes | **Tax** (on the website) → pick the year → Download |
| Ask the app a question | **Assistant / AI →** type your question |

---

*That's the whole system in everyday terms. If something doesn't look right or you're not sure
what a button does — tap it; nothing breaks, and everything can be undone.*
