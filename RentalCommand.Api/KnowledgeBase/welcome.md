Rental Command is a property-management app for landlords who run their business from a phone and a stack of paper. It keeps track of your properties, tenants, leases, rent, and expenses — but it's built to feel simple, not like accounting software.

## The one idea: the computer does the typing for you

Most of the work in property management is copying numbers off a piece of paper into a form. Rental Command flips that around. You **scan, photograph, or talk**, and the app reads the document and fills in the fields. You just **check it and confirm**.

- Snap a photo of a **receipt or bill** and it becomes an expense.
- Photograph a **rent check** and it becomes a payment.
- Upload a **lease** and it becomes a lease record.
- Record a quick **voice note** ("the furnace at 12 Oak is making noise") and it becomes a maintenance request.

Nothing is saved until you say so. The app only ever creates a **draft** for you to review.

## What you can do here

- Track **properties and units** and see who's occupied.
- Keep **tenants, applications, and leases** in one place.
- Log **rent payments and expenses**, and see your money at a glance.
- Connect a **bank feed** and match it to your records.
- Handle **maintenance, inspections, and appointments**.
- Get a plain-English **daily briefing** and **ask questions** about your portfolio.
- Give tenants a **portal** to pay rent and send requests.
