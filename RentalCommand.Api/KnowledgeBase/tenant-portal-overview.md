If your landlord uses Rental Command, you get a **tenant portal** — one place to pay rent, ask for repairs, message your landlord, and see your lease. This article is for **tenants**.

## Your dashboard

When you log in, **My Home** shows the essentials: unread **messages**, anything **overdue**, your **next rent** (how many days until it's due), and your **open maintenance requests**. Notifications from management appear at the top.

## Pay rent

1. Open **Payments**.
2. Each charge shows what it's for, the amount, the due date, and its status. Tap **"What is this charge?"** for a plain-English explanation.
3. On a charge you still owe, click **Pay now** to pay securely online.

**Autopay:** click **Set up autopay** to have rent charged automatically when it's due, so you never miss it. You can **turn it off** any time. (If online payments aren't set up yet, you'll see a note — keep paying the way you do today until it's ready.)

## Request maintenance

1. Open **Maintenance**.
2. Fill in a **title** and **description**, pick a category and priority, and **Submit Request**.
3. Your open requests are listed with their status so you can track them.

## Message your landlord

Open **Messages** to start a conversation or reply to one. Threads show the latest message, and opening a thread marks it read.

## View your lease and ask about it

Open **Lease** to see your lease details — rent, end date, and more. There's also **Ask This Lease**: type a question like "Can I have a pet?", "When is rent due?", or "What's the late fee?" and get an answer drawn from your own lease.
