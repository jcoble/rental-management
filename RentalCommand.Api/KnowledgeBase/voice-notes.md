Sometimes the easiest input isn't a document — it's just talking. On the **Scan** page you can record a **voice note** and Rental Command turns what you said into a draft.

## Record a voice note

1. Open **Scan a Document**.
2. Click **Record voice note** and allow microphone access if your browser asks.
3. Speak naturally — for example, "The furnace at 12 Oak Street is making a loud banging noise, tenant says it started last night."
4. Click **Stop recording**.
5. The app transcribes it and creates a **draft** for you to review.

## Great for maintenance

Voice notes are ideal for capturing maintenance issues while you're standing in front of the problem. The draft opens with the **transcript** shown alongside the extracted details, so you can confirm it into a **work order** without typing.

## Review and confirm

Like any scan, a voice note becomes a draft first, not a finished record. Open it, pick the property if needed, check the details, and **Confirm**. See *Reviewing drafts*.

## If recording isn't available

Voice recording uses your device's microphone through the browser. If your browser doesn't support it or mic access is blocked, you'll get a message — just type the note in instead, or use a photo.
