The **Daily Briefing** is your morning rundown: a short, plain-English list of what needs attention across the whole portfolio, so you don't have to go digging.

## Where to find it

- **Dashboard** — a **Today's Briefing** card with the top items.
- **AI Assistant** page — the full briefing with the date and a refresh button.

## What's in it

The briefing gives a one-line **summary**, then a handful of **bullets**, each with a title, a short detail, and a **severity**:

- **Critical** — needs attention now (e.g. an emergency work order).
- **Warning** — should be handled soon (e.g. rent past due, a lease expiring).
- **Info** — worth knowing.

If nothing needs attention, it simply says **"All clear — nothing needs attention today."**

## Refresh it

On the AI page, click the **refresh** icon to regenerate the briefing. The bullets are drawn from your real data (overdue rent, open maintenance, expiring leases, and so on), so they work even without AI configured; when AI is on, you also get a smarter written summary.

## Get it sent to you

You can have the briefing **delivered** by text or email once a day instead of checking the app. Turn this on in **Settings** under notification delivery, where you set the send hour and the SMS/email recipients. See *Settings and notifications*.
