On the **AI Assistant** page you can **ask questions about your own data** in plain English and get an answer back — no reports to build, no filters to set.

## Ask a question

1. Open the **AI Assistant** page.
2. Type a question in the **Ask a Question** box and press Enter (or tap send).
3. The assistant looks at your portfolio and answers in the chat.

You can keep the conversation going — follow-up questions understand what you just asked.

## Things you can ask

Try the suggested prompts or your own, for example:

- "Who's late on rent?"
- "How much did I collect this month?"
- "Any leases expiring soon?"
- "What needs maintenance?"

## How it answers

The assistant pulls from your real records — payments, leases, work orders, and more. Under an answer it may show **"Looked at: …"** listing the data it checked, so you can see where the answer came from.

## If the assistant is off

The Q&A needs AI to be configured for your account. If it isn't, you'll see a note that **AI is off** and the chat won't answer — but your **Daily Briefing** bullets still work without it.
