The **Appointments** page is your calendar for anything that needs a time slot — showings, move-ins, inspections, and more.

## Views

Appointments opens in a **Calendar** view by default, with a **List** view you can switch to. The calendar shows everything color-coded by type so your week is readable at a glance.

## Schedule an appointment

1. Open **Appointments**.
2. Click **New** (or click an empty slot on the calendar to prefill that time).
3. Give it a **title** and pick a **type**:
   - **Showing** — a prospect viewing a unit
   - **Move-In** / **Move-Out**
   - **Inspection**
   - **Maintenance Visit**
   - **Owner Meeting**
4. Set the **start** and **end** times.
5. Optionally link a **property**, a **tenant**, or a **prospect** (name and email), and assign it to someone.
6. Set a **status** (Scheduled, Confirmed, Completed, Cancelled, No-Show) and save.

## Reschedule by dragging

On the calendar you can **drag an appointment** to a new day or time to reschedule it — no need to reopen the form. If a change can't be saved it snaps back.

## On your dashboard

**Upcoming Appointments** appear on the Dashboard so the next showing or move-in is always in view.
