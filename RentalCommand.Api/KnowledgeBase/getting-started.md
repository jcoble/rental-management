This is the quickest path from a brand-new account to a working portfolio.

## Explore first if you want

When you log in, the **Dashboard** is your home base. It shows your money snapshot, occupancy, overdue rent, open work orders, today's briefing, and recent activity. Click around the left-hand menu — nothing breaks just from looking, and you can delete anything you add while you're learning.

## Set up your real portfolio

When your portfolio is empty, the Dashboard shows a **"Finish setting up your portfolio"** banner. Click **Start setup** to open the guided wizard, which walks you through it one step at a time:

1. **Portfolio** — name your portfolio and set your time zone.
2. **Owner** — add the owner (you, or an LLC/co-owner).
3. **Property** — add your first property and its units.
4. **Tenants** — add the people living there.
5. **Lease** — tie a tenant to a unit with rent terms.

You can leave and come back; the wizard remembers what you've already created and lets you skip ahead.

## You don't have to type everything

Remember the core idea: anywhere you'd normally type a record, you can usually **scan or photograph** the source document instead and let the app fill it in. See **How scanning works** for the big picture.

## Tip: do one property end-to-end

Rather than entering everything at once, set up **one property, one unit, one tenant, one lease**, then log a rent payment and an expense for it. Once you've seen the full loop, the rest is just repetition.
