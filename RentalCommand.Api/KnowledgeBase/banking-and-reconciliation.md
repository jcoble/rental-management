**Banking** brings your real bank activity into Rental Command so you can confirm that the rent and expenses you recorded actually hit your account. It's **read-only** — Rental Command never moves money, it just reads transactions and helps you match them.

## Connect a bank feed

1. Open **Banking**.
2. Click **Connect Plaid** (or **Connect account**) and pick your bank through the secure Plaid window.

Once connected, click **Sync** on the connection to pull in new bank lines. (If you don't use Plaid, you can also import lines manually.) **Deposits** show as positive amounts, **withdrawals** as negative.

## Match a bank line — one tap

For each unmatched bank line, Rental Command suggests the payment or expense it probably belongs to. Matching is a **one-tap confirm**: click **Match** to link them. There's also a **Review suggested matches** queue at the top — for deposits that look like rent you already recorded, click **Confirm match** so the same money isn't counted twice (or **Not a match** to dismiss).

## The "✓ Cleared" badge

Once a payment or expense is matched to a bank line, it shows a green **✓ Cleared** badge in your Accounting ledger — your proof that the money actually moved. In the ledger you may also see a **"Match?"** chip on a record when a likely bank line is found; tap it to clear it right there.

## Ignore personal lines

Bank feeds include personal spending (coffee, rideshare) that has nothing to do with the rentals. Click **Ignore** on those lines to keep them **out of your books**. They move to the **Ignored** filter and can be brought back with **Un-ignore** if you change your mind.
