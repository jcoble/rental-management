**Inspections** let you schedule a walkthrough of a property and work through a checklist of items — useful for move-ins, move-outs, and routine safety checks.

## Schedule an inspection

1. Open **Maintenance & Inspections**.
2. Click **Inspection**.
3. Pick the **property**.
4. Choose a **type** — Routine, Move-In, Move-Out, or Annual Safety.
5. Optionally pick a **checklist** template to load a ready-made list of items to check. (Choosing a checklist sets a matching type for you.)
6. Optionally name the **inspector** and set a **scheduled date and time**.
7. Click **Schedule**.

You're taken straight into the checklist so you can start ticking items off.

## Checklist templates

Templates give you a standard set of items so every inspection of a given type is consistent. Some are **built-in**; you can use those as-is or pick **No checklist (blank)** to start empty.

## Track them

Scheduled inspections appear in the **Inspections** list on the Maintenance page, each showing its type, property, status, and scheduled time. Click one to open its checklist and record results.

## Tip: pair with appointments

Many inspections also belong on your calendar. See *Appointments* to put a move-in or inspection on the schedule alongside showings.
