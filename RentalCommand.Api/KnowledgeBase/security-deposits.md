A **security deposit** is the tenant's money you're **safekeeping**, not income you've earned. Rental Command tracks deposits on a dedicated **Deposits** page, separate from rent and other income on the Money page.

## Why it's its own page

Rent and other payments are income and live in **Money**. A deposit is held **in trust** until the rental ends — it remains the tenant's money unless a documented deduction applies. The Deposits page shows what you received, what you deducted or refunded, and how much you still hold.

## Find the tenant's deposit

Rental Command prepares a deposit account as part of move-in. You do not need to create a second holding.

1. Open **Deposits**.
2. Search for the tenant, property, or unit.
3. Select the row to open its deposit details.

The list shows the amount received, deductions, current status, and the amount still held.

## Record money you received

Opening a deposit account does not mean the tenant has paid it. Record the money when you actually receive it:

1. Open the tenant's deposit details.
2. Select **Record funds**.
3. Enter the amount, received date, payment method, and an optional check or confirmation number.
4. Select **Record funds** to save.

## Add a deduction

When a documented cost can be taken from the deposit:

1. Open the tenant's deposit details and select **Add deduction**.
2. Enter a **reason** (e.g. "Carpet cleaning") and an **amount**, plus optional notes.
3. Enter the deduction date and save.

Deductions reduce the amount still held. Keep receipts, photos, and notes that support each deduction.

## Return the deposit

When you return some or all of the deposit:

1. Open the tenant's deposit details and select **Return the deposit**.
2. Enter the refund date. Leave the amount blank to refund the full amount still held, or enter a smaller amount for a partial refund.
3. Add a check or confirmation number if you have one, then select **Record refund**.

The deposit summary keeps the received amount, deductions, refunds, and current held balance together.

## Prepare the move-out record

Use **Add photo** to attach condition photos. Select **Move-out statement** to download the current deposit record for the tenant. Review local deadlines and required wording before sending it; Rental Command helps organize the record but does not provide legal advice.
