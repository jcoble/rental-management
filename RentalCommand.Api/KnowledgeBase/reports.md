The **Reports** page is a catalog of every report Rental Command can produce, grouped by topic — accounting, rent and payments, owners, and operations.

## Run a report

1. Open **Reports**.
2. Browse the cards (e.g. Income & Expense Statement, Property P&L, General Ledger, Cash Flow, Rent Roll, Rent Ledger, Delinquency, Occupancy, Lease Expirations, Security Deposit Register, Vendor 1099, Work Orders).
3. Click a report to open it.
4. Set the **date range** and choose **properties**, then **export or print**.

## Reports that open existing pages

A few reports are marked **"Opens existing"** because they already have a dedicated home:

- **Schedule E** and **Year-End Packet** open the **Tax** page.
- **Owner Statement** opens the **Owner Reports** page.

## Need a report you don't see?

At the bottom there's a **"Need a report you don't see?"** box. Describe what you're trying to figure out (for example, "every late fee charged this year by property") and submit it. It's logged as a request so the report can be considered for a future update.
