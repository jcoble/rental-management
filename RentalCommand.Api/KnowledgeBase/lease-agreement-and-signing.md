Once a lease is set up, Rental Command can produce the actual **lease agreement document** and send it to your tenant to sign electronically — no printing or scanning required.

## Generate the lease agreement PDF

1. Open the lease detail page.
2. Click **Generate** to create the lease agreement PDF from the lease's terms.
3. Once generated, a **Download** button appears so you can save or print a copy.

You can regenerate it if you change the lease terms.

## Send for electronic signature

1. On the lease, click **Send for signature**.
2. The tenant gets the agreement to sign **electronically** — they don't need to print, sign, and scan anything.

The signature card shows the current status: **Not sent**, **Sent — waiting for signature**, **Signed**, or **Declined**. While it's out for signature the page checks automatically and flips to **Signed** once the tenant signs. After that, a **Download signed lease** option gives you the finished, signed PDF.

## If signing isn't available

Electronic signature relies on an e-signature provider being set up for your account. If it isn't, you'll see a calm note that it's "not configured" instead of an error. You can still generate and download the PDF and have it signed the traditional way.
