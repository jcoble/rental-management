Simple and Advanced are two ways to **view the same books**. Switching modes does not change a payment, charge, balance, report, or accounting record.

## Simple mode

Simple mode uses the words you already use to run your rentals. It shows what increased, what decreased, and what the current balance means.

Choose Simple if you:

- manage your own rentals;
- are moving from spreadsheets;
- want quick answers about cash, rent owed, expenses, and tenant balances; or
- do not need account codes and debit or credit columns during daily work.

You still get complete accounting records. Simple mode only removes details that are not needed for the task in front of you.

## Advanced mode

Advanced mode shows account codes, debit and credit columns, posting dates, journal references, and other details used to review the books.

Choose Advanced if you:

- work with a bookkeeper or accountant;
- are reviewing how a transaction was recorded;
- need to trace a report total back to its accounting lines; or
- already understand double-entry bookkeeping.

Advanced mode is a detail view, not a separate set of books.

## What never changes

Both modes use the same recorded charges, payments, credits, expenses, deposits, and accounting entries. A tenant's balance and your financial statements do not change when you switch modes.

Use Simple for day-to-day work. Switch to Advanced when you or your accountant need to inspect the bookkeeping details.
