The **Scan** page is where you hand the app a document instead of typing. Upload a photo or PDF and Rental Command reads it, then asks you to confirm a draft.

## Scan a document

1. Open **Scan a Document**.
2. Under **"What are you scanning?"** pick the type:
   - **Receipt / Bill** → becomes an **expense**
   - **Rent Check / Payment** → becomes a **payment**
   - **Maintenance Request** → becomes a **work order**
   - **Lease Agreement** → becomes a **lease**
3. Drag in (or take) a **photo or PDF**.
4. The app uploads it and opens the draft. While it reads the document you'll see **"Reading your document…"**; this usually takes a few seconds.
5. Review the extracted fields and **Confirm**. See *Reviewing drafts*.

## What each type needs from you

- **Receipt / Bill** — confirm the amount and category; mark it **already paid** or an **unpaid bill**. You can attach it to a property.
- **Rent Check / Payment** — choose **which lease** the payment is for.
- **Maintenance Request** — choose **which property** the work order is for.
- **Lease Agreement** — choose the **property** and **unit** (required) and a **tenant** (or let it create one from the lease).

## Your scan list

Below the upload area, every scan is listed with its status — **Processing**, **Ready to review**, **Confirmed**, **Failed**, or **Rejected**. Use the tabs to filter. Click any row to review it; a confirmed scan links straight to the record it created.

## Bulk import leases

Have a stack of existing leases? Click **Bulk import leases** to upload several at once and review them together as a batch. Recent imports show as quick links back into review.
